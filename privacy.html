Privacy Policy

OfflineAICalculator · Effective 29 September 2026

In one sentence: OfflineAICalculator collects nothing, shares nothing and makes no network connections — your calculations, your questions and everything the AI does with them stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system and no servers, and it contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. We — the developer — never receive your calculations, questions, history or any information about you or your device.

What the app keeps on your device

WhatWhere it is keptWho can see it
History — finished sums, Ask answers and saved tool results, with your pins and labelsA file in the app’s own storage on your device, with a backup copy beside itOnly you
Memory (M+, M−), the last answer (Ans) and variables you defineThe app’s own storage on your deviceOnly you
Preferences — theme, angle unit, number format, fractions, live answer, haptics, AI on or off, history saving and how long history is keptThe app’s settings on your deviceOnly you

If you turn history saving off, new calculations are not kept. History older than the period you choose is removed automatically; pinned entries are always kept. Questions you type in Ask are shown in the conversation while the app is open; answered calculations are kept in History as above.

The on-device AI

The AI model is part of the app and runs on your device’s own processor. Your questions are processed on the device and nowhere else — there is no server, no cloud service and nothing is downloaded. You can switch the AI off in Settings; the calculator, the plain-English answers and every tool keep working without it.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or sync.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, profiling or crash reporting.
  • No network connections of any kind — the app works in flight mode.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, calendar or health data. The app asks for no permissions.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone — there is no data to share.

When you share

If you use Copy, Share or Export (text or CSV), the content goes wherever you choose to send it, using iOS’s own share options. The app does not see or keep anything about what happens after that.

Deleting your data

In the app you can delete a single history entry, several at once, or clear everything (or everything except pinned entries); a deletion can be undone until the app is closed. Deleting the app removes its history, settings, memory and variables from your device. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
